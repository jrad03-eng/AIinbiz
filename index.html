What Decade Golf does

Everything your game
needs, powered by AI

Stop entering data manually and start getting insights automatically. Decade Golf's AI learns your game over time and serves up the decisions that matter.

🗣️
Natural language input

Describe your round in plain English — "Shot 79 at Northampton, hit 10 fairways, 3-putted twice" — and AI parses every stat automatically. No tedious forms.

🤖
AI-powered insights

Your personal caddie surfaces the single biggest scoring opportunity after every round — not a wall of data, just the one thing you need to focus on next.

📊
Personalized dashboard

A dynamic dashboard that adapts to your game. Your key stats, trends, and milestones — presented in a layout that surfaces what matters to your handicap.

🏌️
Strategy recommendations

Before you tee off, get hole-by-hole strategy built around your tendencies. Which side of the fairway to favor, when to layup, where your short game excels.

📈
Trend analysis

Track long-term improvement across dozens of stats. See exactly where you've gained strokes and where the next gains are hiding — with historical context.

🎯
Practice planning

AI builds your next practice session around your actual weaknesses, not generic drills. Specific yardages, specific shot types — tailored to what will lower your score fastest.
